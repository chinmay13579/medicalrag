# Research Data Access

## Request Workflow
Researchers submit a data request stating the purpose, variables, cohort definition and legal basis. Requests are checked by the Data Access Committee, which responds within 20 business days. Each approved request receives a unique permit that expires at the end of the project.

## Ethics and Agreements
A positive opinion from an ethics committee is required for any request involving individual-level data. The researcher's institution must sign a data use agreement that forbids re-identification and onward sharing.

## Secure Processing Environment
Individual-level data is available only inside the Secure Processing Environment. Researchers cannot download raw rows or copy data out. Only aggregated results that satisfy the small numbers rule leave the environment, after an output check by a data steward.

## Opt-Outs and Exclusions
Before any extract is built the opt-out register is applied and patients who opted out of secondary use are removed. Records under legal restriction and genetic data require additional written approval from the Data Protection Officer.
