# Data Residency and Cross-Border Transfers

## Hosting Regions
Slovak and Czech patient data is hosted only in EU data centres in Frankfurt and Prague. Indian patient data is hosted in the Mumbai region and does not leave India. Each region runs its own encryption keys managed in a regional hardware security module.

## Transfers Between the EU and India
Identifiable health data is not transferred between the EU and India. Only anonymised aggregates, and pseudonymised data covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and a completed transfer impact assessment, may cross the border. Each transfer needs written approval from the Data Protection Officer.

## Cross-Border Care in the EU
When a Czech patient is treated in Slovakia, the treating clinician retrieves the patient summary and ePrescription through the national contact points of the MyHealth@EU infrastructure. The Platform does not copy the record into the foreign country; access is read-only and logged.

## Subprocessors
Subprocessors must be approved by the Data Protection Officer and listed on the public subprocessor page. Customers receive 30 days notice before a new subprocessor is added.
