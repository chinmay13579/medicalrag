# Incident Response

## Incident Types
A data breach is any loss, unauthorised access or disclosure of personal data. A clinical safety incident is any defect that could lead to patient harm, such as a medication list showing the wrong patient. Both are reported through the same on-call channel but follow different playbooks.

## Severity and Timing
Severity 1 covers a confirmed exposure of identifiable health data or a wrong-patient record; the on-call engineer must respond within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day. Severity 2 covers a suspected exposure or degraded interoperability for one provider and gets a response within 1 hour. Severity 3 is everything else and is handled the next business day.

## Notification Duties
The care provider, as controller, must be informed without undue delay and no later than 24 hours after the Platform becomes aware of a breach. The controller notifies the supervisory authority within 72 hours. In India, the Data Protection Board and the affected patients are notified as the DPDP Act requires.
