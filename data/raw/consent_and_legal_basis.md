# Consent and Legal Basis

## Primary Use of Health Data
Processing of patient health data for treatment is based on the care provider's legal obligation under national law and on GDPR Article 9(2)(h). The Platform acts as a processor for the care provider and never as controller of treatment records. Patient consent is not required to exchange records between treating clinicians, but a patient may ask the provider to restrict access to specific documents.

## Secondary Use and Opt-Out
Secondary use of health data for research, statistics and public-interest purposes follows the European Health Data Space rules. Patients in Slovakia and Czechia can opt out of secondary use at any time through the patient portal, and the opt-out takes effect within 24 hours. The opt-out register is checked before every research extract is released.

## Patients in India
For patients in India, processing relies on explicit, itemised consent captured through the ABDM consent manager. Each consent artefact names the purpose, the data types and an expiry date. The Platform stops fetching records the moment a consent expires or is revoked.

## Minors and Representatives
Records of patients under 16 in Slovakia and Czechia, and under 18 in India, are accessible only to a legal guardian or an authorised clinician. Guardian status must be verified by the care provider before the Platform grants portal access.

## Patient Rights Requests
Requests for access, correction or deletion arrive at privacy@platform.example and are forwarded to the care provider, who is the controller, within 3 business days. The Platform supports the provider in answering within the statutory one-month deadline.
